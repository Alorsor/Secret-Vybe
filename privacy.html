Last updated: November 14, 2026

Welcome to Secret Vibe. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Secret Vibe collects, uses, protects and handles information when you use our website.

When you use Secret Vibe, we may collect:

Secret Vibe allows users to receive anonymous messages. The recipient is not shown the sender's identity through the normal anonymous messaging feature.

However, anonymous messaging does not guarantee that technical information cannot exist. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate, host and secure the website.

Users are responsible for the content they submit. Do not post passwords, financial information, private addresses or other sensitive personal information.

We may review, restrict or remove content that violates our rules or creates safety concerns.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the website.

Users who want their account or personal information deleted may contact us using the contact details provided below. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

Secret Vibe is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
